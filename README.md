### Why me

I came to AI through testing. My last stretch at **EPAM** was building agents that generated test cases straight from functional requirements documents — the first system I'd worked on that wrote artifacts instead of checking mine. That's why I moved into AI/ML full time, and the testing habit came with me.

Most LLM products ask a model to be honest in a prompt and hope. [job-os](https://jobs.hemnaath.tech) enforces it in code: unverified numbers are stripped after generation, new entities are rejected outright, and a resume version can't be finalized until it clears a deterministic score with zero blocking issues. That's a test suite pointed at a model, and it's the work I want to do.

Everything below runs on infrastructure I deploy and maintain. None of it is a demo notebook.

#### 💼 [job-os](https://github.com/hemnaath04/job-os) — *"Track every application. Tailor every resume. Never lie on your CV."*
A job-search platform whose resume agent physically cannot write a claim that isn't traceable to a verified fact. Grounding is enforced by a post-generation validation pass, not by asking the model nicely.
**Python · FastAPI · LangGraph · PostgreSQL + pgvector · Docker**
